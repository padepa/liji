你的训练数据默认留在设备里

适用于力迹 1.3 与 1.4。1.4 新增功能的条款仅在你安装该版本并使用相关功能时适用。更新：2026 年 10 月 16 日。

中文 · English

我们处理的数据与存储位置

力迹允许你记录动作、重量、次数、训练与动作时长、休息时间、备注、模板和计划。备注可能包含你主动填写的身体状态、疼痛感受或动作体验。这些内容保存在设备本地数据库；力迹不创建账号，不提供跨设备云同步，也不会把训练记录上传至开发者服务器。

主题、语言及训练提醒等偏好保存在本机。当前版本没有广告、第三方统计 SDK 或跨 App 用户追踪，不读取或写入 Apple 健康数据，不包含 Apple Watch App。

训练提醒与 1.4 休息通知

每周训练提醒使用 iOS 本地通知，由你选择训练日、时间及是否开启。从 1.4 起，还可选择休息结束通知；该选项默认关闭，主动开启时才请求系统通知权限。如果此前已允许训练提醒，iOS 可能不再重复弹出权限请求。

休息结束通知只提示准备继续训练，不显示动作、重量、次数或备注。暂停计时、修改休息时长或提前继续时会撤销旧的休息提醒。你可关闭 App 内相应选项，或在 iOS 设置管理通知。系统设置、专注模式及系统调度可能影响展示；没有开发者推送服务器或第三方通知 SDK。

1.4 锁屏实时活动

组间休息可通过 Apple 系统实时活动显示在锁屏和支持机型的灵动岛。只展示通用休息状态与时间，不包含动作名称、重量、次数、备注或完整训练记录。你可以在休息页“锁屏与提醒”关闭锁屏倒计时，也可通过系统设置管理实时活动。锁屏内容可能被看到你的设备屏幕的人看到。

1.4 桌面小组件

主 App 与自有组件通过本机共享容器交换最小摘要：训练状态、计划或训练日名称、目标动作或组数、语言和主题。摘要不包含训练记录 ID、重量、实际次数、备注或完整数据库，不上传服务器。共享容器不会向无关 App 开放训练数据库。

组件只导航进入 App，不自动开始训练或记组，刷新时机由 iOS 控制。摘要是可重建缓存，排除系统备份。你可以移除组件以停止桌面展示；计划名称等摘要可能被看到你的设备屏幕的人看到。

免费版本与旧订阅

1.3 与 1.4 的已开放训练功能免费，不提供新的应用内购买或订阅入口。旧 Pro 订阅由 Apple 和你的 Apple 账户管理，力迹不读取或保存银行卡号等付款信息。

如曾订阅，请在 Apple 订阅设置核对续费状态。退款申请可前往 Apple 报告问题；本政策不保证特定退款结果。

主动分享与支持

只有你主动打开系统分享面板时，所选训练或 PR 海报才会交给你选择的目标 App。力迹不会在后台自动分享或上传。分享目的地按其自身政策处理收到的内容。

如主动通过邮件联系支持，我们会收到你在邮件中提供的地址和内容，用于处理该问题。请勿发送不必要的健康隐私、设备密码或验证码；邮件由你所用的邮件服务处理。

删除与系统备份

从设备删除 App 会删除当前本地训练记录。力迹不运营账号或云备份；如果你启用了 iCloud 或电脑整机备份，iOS 可能按系统设置把 App 数据纳入备份，备份与恢复由 Apple 和你的设置管理。开发者无法从自己的服务器恢复这些记录。桌面摘要缓存不进入系统备份。

政策更新与联系

开发者品牌：渐成造物 / Gradform Studio。数据处理方式改变时，我们会更新政策及日期。如有隐私问题，请联系 gradformstudio@outlook.com。

Your workout data stays on your device by default

This policy covers Liji 1.3 and 1.4. Terms for features added in 1.4 apply only after you install that version and use those features. Updated October 16, 2026.

Data and storage

Liji stores exercises, weight, reps, workout and set duration, rest time, notes, templates, and plans in a local database on your device. Notes may contain physical condition or pain information you choose to enter. Liji does not create an account, offer cross-device cloud sync, or upload workout records to a developer-operated server.

Preferences such as theme, language, and workout reminders stay on the device. These versions contain no ads, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. They do not read or write Apple Health data or include an Apple Watch app.

Workout reminders and rest alerts in 1.4

You choose the days, time, and whether to enable weekly workout reminders, which use iOS local notifications. From 1.4, optional rest completion alerts are also available. Rest alerts are off by default and request system notification permission only when enabled. iOS may not ask again if you already granted notification access for workout reminders.

Rest alerts provide a generic prompt to continue, without exercise names, weight, reps, or notes. Pausing, changing the rest duration, or continuing early cancels the previous rest alert. You can turn off the relevant option in Liji or manage notifications in iOS Settings. System settings, Focus, and system scheduling can affect presentation. No developer-operated push server or third-party notification SDK is used.

Rest Live Activities in 1.4

Apple system Live Activities can show generic rest status and time on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island on supported devices. They do not include exercise names, weight, reps, notes, or full workout records. Turn off Lock Screen Countdown from Lock Screen & Alerts on the rest page, or manage Live Activities in system settings. People who can see your device screen may see this content.

Home Screen widgets in 1.4

The app and its own widget exchange a minimal summary through an on-device shared container: workout status, plan or training-day names, target exercise or set counts, language, and theme. It contains no record IDs, weight, completed reps, notes, or full database and is not uploaded. The shared container does not expose the workout database to unrelated apps.

Widgets only open Liji; they do not start a workout or log a set for you. iOS controls refresh timing. The rebuildable summary cache is excluded from system backups. Remove widgets to stop Home Screen display. People who can see your device screen may see plan names and other displayed summary content.

Free versions and previous subscriptions

Available workout features in 1.3 and 1.4 are free, with no new in-app purchase or subscription entry. Previous Pro subscriptions are managed by Apple through your Apple Account. Liji does not receive or store card numbers.

Check renewal status in Apple subscription settings. Refund requests go through Report a Problem; this policy does not promise a particular refund outcome.

Sharing and support

A workout or PR poster is sent to your selected destination only when you open the system share sheet. Liji does not share or upload it automatically. The receiving app processes shared content under its own policy.

If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to include to handle your request. Do not send unnecessary health details, device passwords, or verification codes. Your chosen email service processes the message.

Deletion and backups

Deleting the app deletes its current local workout records. Liji does not operate accounts or cloud backups. iOS may include app data in an iCloud or computer device backup according to your settings; Apple manages that process. We cannot recover these records from a developer server. Widget summary caches are excluded from system backups.

Changes and contact

Developer brand: Gradform Studio. We will update the policy and date if our data practices change. Contact gradformstudio@outlook.com with privacy questions.
